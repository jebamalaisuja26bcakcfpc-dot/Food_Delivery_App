🍔 Foodie – Food Delivery App
📌 Project Overview
Foodie is a user-friendly food delivery application designed to make ordering food simple, quick, and convenient. The application allows users to search for restaurants and food items, explore menus, add items to the cart, enter delivery details, and place orders easily.
The main focus of this project is to provide a simple and attractive user experience with an easy food-ordering process.
🎯 Objectives
The main objectives of the Foodie Food Delivery App are:
To provide a simple and user-friendly platform for ordering food online.
To help users quickly search for their favourite food items and restaurants.
To display different food categories such as Pizza, Burger, Noodles, Biryani, Pasta, Sandwiches, Desserts, and Drinks.
To allow users to explore popular restaurants and their menus.
To provide detailed information about food items, including price, offers, and descriptions.
To allow users to add multiple food items to the shopping cart.
To provide an easy and organized cart management system.
To allow users to enter and manage their delivery address.
To show estimated delivery time before placing the order.
To provide offers and discounts to make food ordering more attractive.
To provide a smooth and simple checkout process.
To give users a clear confirmation after successfully placing an order.
To improve the overall food-ordering experience through a clean and attractive UI design.
✨ Key Features
🔍 Search
Users can search for food items and restaurants easily.
🍕 Food Categories
The app provides different categories such as:
Pizza
Burger
Noodles
Biryani
Pasta
Sandwich
Desserts
Drinks
🏪 Restaurant Listing
Users can explore popular restaurants and view their ratings, food type, delivery time, and offers.
🍽️ Restaurant Menu
Users can open a restaurant and view the available food items with their prices, descriptions, and discounts.
🛒 Add to Cart
Users can add their favourite food items to the cart and adjust the quantity.
📦 Cart
The cart displays selected items, quantities, prices, delivery charges, discounts, and total amount.
📍 Delivery Address
Users can provide their delivery location/address for receiving the order.
⏱️ Estimated Delivery Time
The application displays an estimated delivery time to help users know when their order may arrive.
🎁 Offers & Discounts
Special offers and discounts are displayed for selected food items and restaurants.
💳 Checkout & Payment
Users can review their order details and proceed with the payment/order process.
✅ Order Confirmation
After successfully placing the order, the app displays an Order Placed Successfully confirmation screen.
👤 User Flow
Home → Search → Select Restaurant → View Menu → Add to Cart → Cart → Delivery Address → Checkout/Payment → Order Confirmation
🎨 UI/UX Design
The application uses a modern dark-themed interface with attractive food images, clear typography, buttons, cards, and organized navigation.
The design focuses on:
Simplicity
Easy navigation
Consistency
Visual appeal
User-friendly interaction
Clear information presentation
